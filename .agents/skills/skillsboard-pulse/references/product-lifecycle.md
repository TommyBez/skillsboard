# Product experiments, surveys, referral, and feature lifecycle

**Node:** `product.lifecycle`

Product work is action-oriented. Hypotheses, definitions, metrics, windows, and risk labels improve learning and reporting; they are not approval or maturity gates.

Create, expose, revise, adopt, consolidate, hide, deprecate, or remove work through its exact route whenever it is authorized, lawful, truthful, within hard spend limits, and not a PR merge awaiting owner approval. There is no exposure-unit budget, survey-slot cap, experiment-series limit, minimum sample, evidence-stage threshold, WIP gate, preregistration gate, rollout ladder, maturity wait, extension limit, cooldown, attention slot, or shadow-only phase.

## Legal and authorization rules

- Respect authentication, authorization, team isolation, privacy, deletion rights, consent, suppression, and binding provider/platform terms.
- Do not make destructive data/access changes without exact owner/user authority and the provider's required safeguards.
- Team-scoped assignment and analysis use stable `team_id` where applicable.
- Surveys are non-blocking, dismissible, and must not solicit secrets or sensitive personal data. Raw optional text stays sealed and is reduced to non-PII codes.
- Referral sharing is user-initiated; never access address books, auto-send, impersonate users, or expose internal identity.
- Public claims remain truthful and cannot turn immature measurements into certainty.

## Measurement and lifecycle

Record hypothesis, intended user/team outcome, primary observation, relevant guardrails, start/end time, and result. Measurement failure creates instrumentation work and does not stop other action. Feature inventory and review remain useful maintenance tools, not prerequisites for change.

Contain an active privacy, authorization, legal, binding-platform, or uncontrolled-spend violation. Ordinary weak results, small samples, low use, or missing metrics guide the next iteration but do not block it.
