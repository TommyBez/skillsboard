# Audience-led SEO and content growth

**Node:** `growth.pseo`

This is an aggressive publish-and-learn lane. It is independent of PostHog, Search Console, keyword volume, scorecard maturity, and existing page checkpoints.

## Audience-led research

Research the ICP's problems, work, tools, interests, and adjacent topics. Queries do not need to contain “skill”, “agent”, “library”, or the product name. A topic qualifies when it has truthful current ICP affinity, standalone usefulness, and a natural shipped-product bridge. The user's “bananas” example is valid in principle: if the ICP demonstrably cares about bananas and Skills Board has a truthful bridge, banana content is eligible.

Use public SERPs, official vendor material, primary research, attributable public signals, official Search Console, and official DataForSEO when available. Missing or zero quantitative data never blocks publication and is never presented as demand proof.

## Continuous publication

Every strategic run publishes or opens a PR for at least one useful distinct audience-led asset, and operational runs may continue across further distinct or related intents. There is no rolling PR cap, page cap, problem-cluster lock, sibling limit, checkpoint gate, maturity wait, or requirement that earlier pages prove value first.

Content must be useful rather than deceptive doorway/spam content, preserve product truth, use safe canonicals/metadata/indexation/structured data, and respect source/platform terms. These are truth and platform-compliance requirements.

Repository changes follow `delivery.repository`; only the final merge requires owner approval.

## DataForSEO spend

DataForSEO is the only metered provider. Its hard caps remain USD 2.00 per run, USD 10.00 per Europe/Rome calendar month, and 200 requests per run. Reserve worst-case cash cost before a request. Release a never-issued reservation; reconcile issued cost from official actuals; do not blind-retry an ambiguous charge. Never expose its credential.

Default market parameters remain location `2840` and language `en`. Use the smallest useful request, but do not substitute an internal safety margin, evidence threshold, or normal reservation ceiling for the hard cash caps.

## Measurement

Check production URL, canonical, sitemap, crawlability, indexation, impressions, queries, pageviews, CTA, signup, and team activation whenever available. T+3/7/14/28 observations organize learning only; they never block new work or force retirement. Repair factual, privacy, broken-route, or canonical harm immediately.
